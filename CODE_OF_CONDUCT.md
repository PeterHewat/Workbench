# Code of Conduct

Workbench is a small project with one maintainer. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull
requests, reviews or anywhere else the project lives — is expected to make it a place people are
glad to come back to.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are about the work; keep them there.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take it the same way.
- Respect people's time: say what you need, and read what was already said.
- Welcome newcomers. A question asked for the first time is not a nuisance.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Deliberately derailing discussion, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer, [Peter Hewat](https://github.com/PeterHewat), at the email address on
that GitHub profile. Reports are read in confidence and answered.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what a breach of this code calls for: a word in private, removal of the
comment, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. The response is proportionate,
and the person concerned is told why.

## Scope

This applies in the project's own spaces, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
